About the studio

Black Salamander Studio is a small independent studio working on multiplayer role playing games and the infrastructure underneath them.

Most of what we build starts from the same question: what does a world feel like when it does not pause for you? That pushes us toward persistent servers, NPCs with memory and motive, and systems that keep running whether or not anyone is watching.

We work across original projects and the modding communities we came out of, because both are good places to find out what actually holds up once real players get their hands on it.
