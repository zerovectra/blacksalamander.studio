Projects

The Bleak Isles: Myronith

An original world, built in Unity 6 to be played alone, with friends, or on a dedicated server.

An original open world role playing game set in the Bleak Isles, an archipelago of drowned coasts, old magic and older grudges. Built from the ground up for singleplayer, co-op and dedicated servers alike, so the world works the same whether you sail it alone or with a crew. Myronith is where it starts.

Silt Strider

A Morrowind server running on our own fork of TES3MP. Live now, and still being tested.

Silt Strider is a persistent multiplayer Morrowind, running on a custom fork of TES3MP with an additional custom living world layer on top. NPCs can hold their own conversations and remember what was said and done, towns change hands, and the world's economy changes with how prosperous towns become. This is an ongoing experiment in what a shared Morrowind can be, and we're pushing the limits as far as we can.

About the studio

Black Salamander Studios is a small independent studio working on multiplayer role playing games and the infrastructure underneath them.

Most of what we build starts from the same question: how can we make the games we love feel even better? That pushes us toward persistent servers, NPCs with memory and motive, and systems that really react to how a player interacts with the space around them.

We're working on original projects and with the very special modding communities we came out of, because both are good places to find out what actually holds up once real players get their hands on it. Our goal is to make the best possible worlds imaginable and to ensure that you, the player, have all the necessary tools to live in them.
